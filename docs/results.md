# Results: published reels

Instagram Reels published on a small wellness account (~160 followers) in Mexico, October–November 2026. Figures come from the account's Instagram insights, copied by hand into a working log; the account name is omitted. Reach on a new, small account is noisy: read this as evidence that the line produces watchable videos and learns from each one, not as evidence of reach.

"Skip rate" is Instagram's share of viewers who swiped away in the first ~3 seconds (lower is better).
